What the numbers mean

  • Gross margin: (sales minus cost of sales) / sales (Few, 2013).
  • Stock turnover: cost of sales / average inventory value (Stats SA, 2022).
  • Dead stock: capital in items with zero sales for over 90 days.
  • Branch discrepancies: one branch below reorder while the other has surplus.

A real South African example

  • Trade enterprises average a 21.7% to 23.6% gross margin, about 6.0% net before tax (Stats SA, 2022).
  • Hardware benchmarks 25% to 30%, so the store's 28.6% target is in line.

Trade-offs and risks

  • Too many charts and colours hide the warnings that matter (Few, 2013).
  • Wrong codes at the till make every figure wrong.
  • Watching only revenue can hide a shrinking margin.

What to do on Monday

  • Review the four cards every Monday at 07:30.
  • Approve the flagged inter-branch transfers before ordering.
  • Mark down anything unsold for over 90 days.
