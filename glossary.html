Glossary

Plain-English meanings of the terms used on this site.

TermPlain meaningPage
API (Application Programming Interface)An agreed method that lets two compatible software applications exchange information automatically.Systems
Bespoke softwareSoftware built specifically for one business or organisation.Make It Fit
Change managementPreparing, supporting and guiding people through a change in how they work.Make It Fit
ConfigureUsing a software package's existing settings to suit a business, such as branch locations, user permissions and VAT rates.Make It Fit
ConsentA voluntary, specific and informed agreement to processing.POPIA
COTS (Commercial Off-the-Shelf)Ready-made software designed for many customers rather than built for one business.Make It Fit
CRM (Customer Relationship Management)A system that stores and manages customer details, orders and enquiries.Systems
CustomiseChanging software or adding developed features to meet a need the standard product does not support.Make It Fit
Data migrationMoving information from existing records into a new system.Make It Fit
Data siloInformation kept in one system that other systems cannot easily reach.Systems
Data subjectThe person the personal information is about.POPIA
Direct cutoverReplacing the old system with the new one in a single step, with no period of parallel running.Make It Fit
Direct marketingApproaching a person to promote goods or services.POPIA
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)A system that connects core activities such as purchasing, stock, sales and accounting.Systems
Fit-gap analysisComparing business requirements against a proposed system to see which needs are supported and which are not.Make It Fit
Information OfficerThe store's head, or a management employee they authorise, who is responsible for POPIA compliance and registered with the Regulator.POPIA
Low-codeBuilding applications with visual tools and pre-built parts, with less manual programming.Make It Fit
Offline modeA system's ability to keep certain functions working when there is no internet, syncing later.Make It Fit
OperatorSomeone who processes information for the store under a contract, such as a cloud provider.POPIA
Parallel runningRunning the old and new systems together for a time to compare results and reduce risk.Make It Fit
Personal informationInformation about an identifiable living person, such as a name with a phone number.POPIA
Phased rolloutIntroducing a new system gradually, such as one module or function at a time.Make It Fit
PilotA limited trial of a new system at one location or group before a wider rollout.Make It Fit
POS (Point of Sale)A system that records sales at the till and can update stock and produce reports.Systems
ProcessingAnything done with personal information: collecting, storing, using, sending or deleting it.POPIA
RequirementA function or condition a system must satisfy to meet a business need.Make It Fit
Responsible partyThe business that decides why and how personal information is processed.POPIA
SaaS (Software as a Service)Software hosted by a provider and used over the internet, usually on a subscription.Make It Fit
SCM (Supply Chain Management)A system that manages the movement of goods between suppliers, branches and customers.Systems
Security compromisePersonal information accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person.POPIA
Special personal informationSensitive data such as health, race, religion or biometrics, which is generally prohibited.POPIA
Systems integrationConnecting separate applications so they can exchange information and support one consistent process.Systems
TAM (Technology Acceptance Model)A model that explains technology acceptance partly through how useful and easy to use people find it.Make It Fit
TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)The full cost of buying, running and maintaining a system over a set period.Make It Fit
Vendor lock-inWhen moving away from a provider becomes hard or costly because the business depends on its services, data formats or processes.Make It Fit
