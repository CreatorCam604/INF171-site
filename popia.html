Customer Data and POPIA

What the privacy law means for the store's notebook, WhatsApp group, slips and staff access.

On this page: Why this matters | Key ideas | In our store | SA example | Trade-offs | On Monday | Sources

Why this matters to you

  • The store is a "responsible party" under POPIA (Act 4 of 2013, consolidated 2021).
  • Paper counts as much as digital: a name plus a number is personal information (s 1, s 3).
  • Risk spots: the open notebook, the customer WhatsApp group, unprotected Excel and the shoebox of slips.

Key ideas in plain English

The eight conditions (s 4(1)): accountability, processing limitation, purpose specification, further processing limitation, information quality, openness, security safeguards, data subject participation. Special data (health, race, religion, biometrics) is generally off-limits (ss 26, 27).

  • Information Officer: the owner registers with the Regulator, a branch manager as Deputy (ss 55, 56).
  • Direct marketing (s 69): no WhatsApp or SMS specials without consent or an existing-customer link; opt-out is not consent since April 2025.
  • Security and breaches (ss 19, 22): reasonable safeguards, and report a breach to the Regulator and customers in writing.
  • Transfers and retention (ss 72, 14): offshore cloud is a transfer; keep records only as long as needed.

  • CCTV: signs up, pointed at tills and yards, not staff break areas.
  • Staff and buying history: collect only what is needed, keep the debt notebook off the counter.

A real South African example

  • FT Rams Consulting (21 Feb 2024): emails without consent breached s 69; an unsubscribe link did not fix it.
  • Department of Justice: first fine, R5 million (3 July 2023). Fines can reach R10 million.

Trade-offs and risks

  • Lawful is not the same as acceptable: would a customer be surprised?
  • Collect less, protect what you keep, be able to explain what you hold.
  • This is an educational resource, not formal legal advice.

What to do on Monday

  • Register the Information Officer and a Deputy.
  • Write a one-page privacy notice and brief all staff.
  • Move specials to a written opt-in and close the open WhatsApp group.
  • Lock the order book, password-protect Excel, keep backups.
  • Agree a retention schedule with the accountant.
