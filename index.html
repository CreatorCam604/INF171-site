Run both branches from one clear picture

Meet BuildRight Hardware

  • Family-owned hardware and building supplies, owner Thabo Dlamini.
  • Two branches: Vanderbijlpark and Vereeniging, about 15 staff.
  • Runs on Excel, a paper order book, a basic till, paper invoices, a WhatsApp group and a monthly shoebox of slips.

The problem

  • Nearly lost a cement sale: 8 bags showing at one branch, 142 at the other, no shared view.
  • Cash stuck in dead stock (some grinders unsold for 115 days).
  • Customer details sit in an open notebook.
  • No clear daily profit, because sales, stock and expenses are kept apart.
