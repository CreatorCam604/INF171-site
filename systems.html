Connecting Your Systems

Why the till, stock and accounting should share information, and how to connect them in steps.

On this page: Why this matters | Key ideas | In our store | Before and after | SA example | Trade-offs | On Monday | Sources

Why this matters to you

  • Separate records mean no shared stock view and no clear daily profit.
  • Connecting them cuts double entry and keeps both branches consistent.

Key ideas in plain English

  • ERP: links purchasing, stock, sales and accounting.
  • CRM: manages customer details and enquiries.
  • SCM: manages goods between suppliers, branches and customers.
  • POS: records sales and updates stock.
  • Accounting: records income, expenses and accounts.
  • Integration: these share information, so a sale is entered once.

What it looks like in our store

  • One shared product list: unique code, standard name, price, per-branch stock.
  • Delivery, sale, reorder alert and transfer all flow from the same records.
  • Start small and connected, add modules in phases, not one big platform at once.

A real South African example

  • Options to compare: Sage, Xero, Odoo and Shopify POS (vendor sites).
  • No need for one big suite: accounting plus a connected inventory or POS app can be enough.
  • Check pricing, local support and features before choosing.

Trade-offs and risks

  • Data silos and duplicate records.
  • APIs vs manual exports, and legacy data to clean first.
  • Vendor lock-in, cost and staff skills.
  • Load-shedding and security (POPIA applies).
