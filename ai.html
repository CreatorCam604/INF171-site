Smarter Decisions with AI

Moving from gut feel to data-supported decisions, without the AI hype.

On this page: Why this matters | Key ideas | In our store | SA example | Trade-offs | On Monday | Sources

Why this matters to you

  • The value is using the data you already have, not a robot running the store.
  • It only works once the data is reliable, so this comes after data quality.

Key ideas in plain English

Analytics maturity

  • Descriptive: what happened (yesterday's sales).
  • Diagnostic: why (margin dipped because of a supplier price rise).
  • Predictive: what is likely (cement demand next week).
  • Prescriptive: what to do (order 60 bags now).

Decision types

  • Structured, semi-structured and unstructured decisions.
  • Operational (daily reorders), tactical (monthly buying), strategic (open a third branch).

Decision support systems (DSS)

  • Data, a model and a simple screen let the owner test "what if we mark down slow stock 15%?".
  • A DSS supports judgement, it does not replace it.

What it looks like in our store

  • Reorder rules from sales history instead of memory.
  • Simple forecasting for the busy pre-December season.
  • A stock-query chatbot on WhatsApp reading live stock.
  • Till anomaly flags for unusual sales or prices.
  • Every suggestion is checked by a person.

A real South African example

  • Xero's 2026 State of South African Small Business report tracks how local SMEs adopt digital and data tools.
  • The lesson: tools help, but only on top of accurate records.

Trade-offs and risks

  • Garbage in, garbage out: poor data gives confident wrong answers.
  • Over-trust: treat a suggestion as a suggestion, not a command.
  • Cost and complexity: start with built-in reports.
  • POPIA s 71 limits decisions made purely by automation, so keep a human in the loop.

What to do on Monday

  • Clean the sales and product data first.
  • Pick one guess-based decision as a test case.
  • Check one forecast against reality for a month before relying on it.
  • Name a person responsible for every automated suggestion.
