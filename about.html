About Us

We are a student consulting team that focuses on practical, affordable information systems advice for South African small businesses. Our work uses a fictional hardware store to show how better data, the right technology and responsible business practices can improve everyday decisions. We try to explain our recommendations clearly and keep in mind the money and time limits that small businesses face.
